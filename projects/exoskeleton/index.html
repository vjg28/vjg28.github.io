Exoskeleton

A wearable rig for collecting egocentric data for robots.

Draft. The write-up for this project is in progress. Sections below are the structure it will follow.

Why build it

Robots learn manipulation well from demonstrations, and the most natural demonstrations come from a person's own point of view. This rig is a way to capture that egocentric data while someone simply does the task.
