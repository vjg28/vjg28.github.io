About

I'm Varshil, an ML scientist from Bharuch, Gujarat, now living in Bengaluru.

At AirMDR I'm building the AI stack that brings top-tier security operations to every organisation, no matter how small or how early: agents that investigate and respond to security alerts on their own. Before that I spent almost four years at Microsoft making sure irrelevant ads don't reach you.

I studied electronics at IIT Guwahati, ran projects at the Robotics Club, and spent my summers on robot learning at Intel Labs, IIT Madras and MILA. That pull towards robots never left. Lately I've been building an exoskeleton for egocentric robot data and CADForge, an RL environment where agents write manufacture-ready CAD.

Most weekends something is printing. I design and 3D-print parts for my own problems.

Outside of that: astrophotography, a garden grown entirely in pots, and roller skating. I raced competitively and won at the national level in 2013.
