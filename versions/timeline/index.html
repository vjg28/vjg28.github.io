Experience, three ways

The same five places in three layouts. Every marker carries its own logo, and the three internships are spread to their real months. Pick one (or mix parts) and it goes on the home page.

Option A — Track

One horizontal axis from 2016 to now. Best for seeing the whole span and the overlaps at a glance; details appear on hover or tap.

Option B — Branches

A git-graph: one main line from college to now, with each internship forking off the college years and merging back. Reads top to bottom, newest first.

Option C — Chapters

Three chapters sized by how long each lasted, with the internships inside college. Calm, scannable, and the impact line is always visible.
