The path so far

  1. Apr 2024 – now

    AirMDR Senior ML Scientist

    Leading AI for an agentic SOC: a self-improving agent harness across 150+ security integrations that investigates alerts on its own. Time to investigate fell from 15 minutes to under 5.

  2. Mar 2023 – Mar 2024

    Microsoft Advertising Data & Applied Scientist 2

    Led query–keyword relevance, the first matching layer of the Ads stack. Replaced external labellers with in-house LLM labelling, saving $500K a year.

  3. Aug 2020 – Mar 2023

    Microsoft Advertising Data & Applied Scientist

    Multilingual, multitask relevance models serving 70+ languages, with a distillation framework that cut compute per query 3×.

  4. 2019 – 2020

    IIT Guwahati Bachelor thesis

    Self-supervised speech representations for Indic code-switched ASR, with Prof. Rohit Sinha.

  5. 2019

    MILA Research intern

    Transformer language models for agents in text-based games, with Dr. Jie Fu.

  6. 2019

    RISE Lab, IIT Madras Research intern

    Graph-based structural bias for generalisable autonomous navigation, with Prof. B. Ravindran.

  7. 2018

    Intel Labs Research intern

    Imitation learning that teaches robot arms from demonstrations, with Dr. Venkat Natarajan.

Studied Electronics & Electrical Engineering at IIT Guwahati, 2016–20, with a minor in Computer Science.
