About

Hi, I'm Varshil.

I'm curious by nature and a builder at heart. I rarely say no to trying something new, and once I start, I go all in. Science and engineering have been part of me for as long as I can remember, and I still build things mostly for the joy of it.

At work, I lead AI at AirMDR, where we're building an AI SOC: agents that run cybersecurity operations on their own. Before that, I spent almost four years at Microsoft training and deploying the language models behind its Ads recommendation system.

At IIT Guwahati, I spent most of my time tinkering with robots and reinforcement learning. That led to internships at some pretty cool places, like Intel Labs, IIT Madras RISE and MILA, and a place in Carnegie Mellon's robotics master's programme, which I passed on when COVID hit.

Robots were my first love, and that's where I'm headed next. I want to spend the next decade on Physical AI.

Outside work, you'll find me gardening, 3D printing something or looking up at the night sky. Sometimes I'm skating or bird watching in Cubbon Park.
